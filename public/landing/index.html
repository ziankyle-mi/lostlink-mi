Where do I drop off a found item?

Submit the found report online to generate your LL reference code, write the code on a tag, and bring the item to Life Science Building Room 102.

Why are student ID photos hidden?

Any report under IDs, Cards and Wallets enables hide_photo automatically so personal student photos stay visible only to Security Admins.

How does claim verification work?

Owners submit at least 20 characters describing unique marks or contents. Security compares the proof against the item held at LSB before pickup.

Who can sign in to LostLink?

Access is limited to verified @dlsau.edu.ph student, faculty, staff, and campus security accounts.
